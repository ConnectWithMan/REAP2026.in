What is REAP Upward Movement?

A second chance to secure your dream college or branch — automatically processed by the system.

REAP Upward Movement is a special round conducted by the Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process after the initial seat allotment rounds. During this round, students who have already reported to an allotted college and opted for upgradation can potentially receive a better seat — a higher-ranked college or a more preferred branch — based on vacancies created by candidates who did not report within the deadline.

The system automatically processes upgradation based on your original merit rank and the order of choices you submitted during choice-filling. You do not need to fill choices again. Your original preferences are used as-is to determine if a higher seat is available for you.

🌟

Automatic Processing

No manual application needed. The system evaluates your higher preferences against available vacant seats automatically based on merit.

🔒

Seat Security

If no upgrade is available, your current allotted seat remains safe and confirmed. It will not be cancelled under any circumstance.

📋

Original Choices Used

Your choice list from the original choice-filling round is used. Higher preferences are checked in exact order of your preference number.

⏳

Strict Deadlines

If upgraded, reporting to the new college within the given deadline is mandatory. Missing it cancels your entire admission.

Eligibility for Upward Movement

Not all candidates are automatically eligible. Verify your status before the round begins.

Criterion Details Status
Reported to allotted college Candidate must have physically reported and completed document verification at the initially allotted institute within the reporting window. Required
Opted for upward movement During reporting, the candidate must have selected the option to participate in upward movement. This is done at the college level and cannot be changed afterwards. Required
Higher preferences exist The candidate must have at least one higher-preference choice during original choice filling that has not yet been allotted to them. Conditional
Valid merit rank Your rank must fall within the cutoff for the upgraded seat (college + branch + category) to be considered for upgradation. Merit-Based
Seat not frozen Candidates who chose to "freeze" their seat during reporting are permanently excluded from all upward movement rounds. Disqualified
Step-by-Step Process

Follow these steps carefully to make the most of the REAP Upward Movement round.

  • 1

    Report to Your Initially Allotted College

    Complete the physical reporting at your allotted institute within the stipulated deadline. Submit all original documents and pay any required security deposit to secure your seat for the upward movement round.

  • 2

    Opt for Upward Movement at the College

    At the time of reporting, explicitly inform the college authority that you wish to participate in upward movement. The institute will mark your preference on the REAP portal. This step is mandatory and cannot be done later.

  • 3

    Wait for Upward Movement Allotment

    REAP processes the upward movement after all reporting closes. Vacant seats (due to non-reporting candidates) are redistributed among eligible upward movement participants based on merit rank and original choice order.

  • 4

    Check Your Upward Movement Result

    Visit the official REAP portal as soon as the result is declared. Log in with your credentials and navigate to the upward movement allotment section on your dashboard.

  • 5

    If Upgraded — Report to the New College

    If you receive an upgraded seat, your old seat is automatically cancelled. Download the new Provisional Allotment Letter (PAL) and report to the newly allotted college within the deadline with all original documents. Failure means your admission is cancelled entirely.

  • 6

    If Not Upgraded — Continue at Current College

    If your seat is not upgraded, no action is required from your side. Your previously allotted seat remains confirmed and finalized. Simply continue your admission process at the original college.

How to Check Upward Movement Result

A simple, step-by-step guide to viewing your result on the official REAP portal.

🌐

Step 1: Go to Official Portal

Visit cegadmissions.co.in and navigate to the REAP 2026 upward movement section.

🔑

Step 2: Log In

Enter your REAP application number and password. Use the same credentials as your initial REAP registration.

📊

Step 3: Check Dashboard

Navigate to the "Upward Movement Allotment" section on your dashboard to see your updated seat status.

📄

Step 4: Download Letter

If upgraded, download the new Provisional Allotment Letter (PAL) and carry it for reporting to your new college.

⚠️

Critical Warning: If your seat is upgraded and you fail to report to the new college within the stipulated deadline, your entire admission will be cancelled — including your original seat. Do not miss the reporting window under any circumstances.

Do’s & Don’ts During Upward Movement

Avoid the most common mistakes that candidates make during the upward movement round.

✅ Do’s

  • Report to your current allotted college within the deadline before the upward movement round.
  • Explicitly opt for upward movement at the college during physical reporting.
  • Keep your login credentials ready to check results as soon as they are declared.
  • Download and print the new allotment letter immediately upon receiving an upgrade.
  • Report to the upgraded college within the stated deadline with all original documents.
  • Keep copies of all receipts, fee payment proofs, and verification documents.
  • Monitor the official REAP portal regularly for schedule updates.

❌ Don’ts

  • Don’t freeze your seat if you want to participate in upward movement — frozen seats are excluded.
  • Don’t wait for the last moment to check your result — check as soon as the result is declared.
  • Don’t ignore an upgraded allotment expecting your old seat to remain — it is automatically cancelled.
  • Don’t forget to carry original documents when reporting to the new college.
  • Don’t assume upgradation is guaranteed — it depends on merit rank and seat availability.
  • Don’t leave the city without completing all college reporting formalities.
