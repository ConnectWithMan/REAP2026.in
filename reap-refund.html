REAP 2026 Refund Guide

Complete Information About Refund Eligibility, Process & Timelines

Refund Overview

The REAP 2026 refund process provides eligible candidates with the opportunity to claim back their ₹10,000 seat acceptance fee under specific circumstances. Understanding the eligibility criteria, refund timeline, and the claim process is essential to ensure you receive your refund promptly.

Refund Eligibility Criteria

You are eligible to claim a refund of your ₹10,000 seat acceptance fee if you fall under any of the following categories:

Who Can Claim Refund?

1

Candidates Who Did Not Report

If you did not complete online reporting (Phase 1) within the specified deadline, you are eligible for a full refund of ₹10,000.

2

Rejection After Physical Verification

If your documents were rejected during physical verification (Phase 2) at the allotted institute, you can claim a refund after the rejection order is finalized.

3

Cancellation Due to Merit Changes

If your status changes to "Special Conditionally" due to a downward revision in merit or change in reservation category, and you are subsequently cancelled, you are eligible for refund.

4

Outright Cancellation

If your seat is cancelled due to ineligibility or violation of REAP rules after fee payment, you can claim a refund with proper documentation.

5

Withdrawal After Allotment

If you withdraw your candidature after receiving seat allotment but before the final round closes, you may be eligible for a refund (subject to specific conditions).

Non-Refundable Scenarios

If you successfully reported online (status: "Reported" or "Conditionally Reported") and did not withdraw, you generally cannot claim a refund, even if you later withdraw from the allotted college. The fee is treated as a seat confirmation payment.

Refund Process & Timeline

The refund process follows a structured timeline to ensure transparent and timely processing of all claims.

Step-by-Step Refund Claim Process

1

Check Your Refund Eligibility

Log in to the REAP portal and check your reporting status. Navigate to the "Refund Status" section to verify if you are eligible for a refund.

2

Collect Required Documents

Gather all necessary documents as per the refund request form: • Bank account details (account holder name, IFSC, account number)
• Cancel cheque or bank passbook copy
• Proof of fee payment (receipt from REAP portal)
• Original seat allotment letter
• Any rejection/cancellation notice from the college

3

Submit Refund Claim Form

Fill out the official REAP refund claim form with accurate bank details and attach all required supporting documents. Submit through the REAP portal within the specified deadline.

4

Verification by REAP Authority

The REAP authority will verify your eligibility and documents. This process may take 5-7 working days from the submission date.

5

Refund Processing

Once approved, your refund will be initiated to your bank account via NEFT/RTGS. Refunds typically take 3-5 business days to reflect in your account.

6

Track Refund Status

Check the REAP portal regularly to track your refund status. You will receive an email/SMS notification once the refund is processed.

⏱️ Important Refund Timelines

  • Refund Claim Submission Deadline: Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the refund-eligible event (rejection, cancellation, etc.)
  • Verification Period: 5-7 working days for document verification
  • Processing Time: 3-5 business days after approval for fund transfer
  • No Claims Accepted After Deadline: Refund requests received after the deadline will be rejected

Documents Required for Refund Claim

Ensure you have all the following documents ready before submitting your refund claim to avoid delays or rejection.

Mandatory Documents

  • REAP Refund Claim Form: Official form available on the REAP portal (duly filled and signed)
  • Proof of Fee Payment: Fee payment receipt/transaction ID from REAP portal
  • Bank Details: Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy showing:
    • Account holder name
    • Account number
    • IFSC code
  • Seat Allotment Letter: Original provisional/final seat allotment letter
  • Proof of Ineligibility: Rejection letter, cancellation notice, or status change document from allotted college/REAP
  • Identity Proof: Aadhar card or PAN card (photocopy)

Important Document Submission Notes

  • All documents should be legible and clear (scanned at minimum 300 DPI)
  • Bank details must match the name in your REAP admission form
  • Cancelled cheque should clearly show bank details; alternatively, submit a bank passbook page showing account details
  • Do NOT submit original documents; only certified copies are accepted
  • Missing or incomplete documents will result in rejection of your refund claim

Frequently Asked Questions About Refunds

❓ Can I get a refund if I don't report for physical verification?

Answer: If you successfully completed online reporting (status: Reported/Conditionally Reported) but fail to report physically, your admission will be cancelled. However, you generally cannot claim a refund as you had already secured the seat. Check your specific REAP rules for exceptions.

❓ What if I withdraw after paying the fee but before online reporting deadline?

Answer: If you withdraw your candidature before completing online reporting, you are eligible for a full refund of ₹10,000. Submit a withdrawal request with proper documentation through the REAP portal.

❓ How long does it take to receive my refund after approval?

Answer: After your refund is approved, it typically takes 3-5 business days for the amount to appear in your bank account via NEFT/RTGS. The timeline may extend during holidays or bank downtime.

❓ What if I don't receive my refund even after 5 business days?

Answer: If the refund doesn't appear in your account after 5 business days:

  • Check your bank account carefully (it might be credited to a different account linked to your bank details)
  • Contact REAP authorities with your transaction reference number
  • Check for any failed refund status in the portal and resubmit if necessary

❓ Is the ₹10,000 fee refundable if I secure admission through upward movement?

Answer: No, the fee is not refundable. The ₹10,000 seat acceptance fee is applicable for your initial allotment and carries forward to subsequent upward movements. You do not need to pay it again, but you cannot claim a refund for it.

❓ Can I claim refund for both rejected seats?

Answer: No, only one refund is allowed. You can only claim refund for your final cancelled/rejected seat. If you had upward movements and the final seat was cancelled, only that cancellation qualifies for refund.

❓ What if my bank details were entered incorrectly in the REAP form?

Answer: If your bank details in the REAP form are incorrect or don't match your passbook:

  • Immediately contact REAP authorities to update your bank details
  • Provide a corrected passbook or cheque when submitting your refund claim
  • Without correction, your refund may be rejected or delayed

❓ What happens if my refund claim is rejected?

Answer: If your refund claim is rejected, you will receive a notification via the portal stating the reason. Common reasons include: missing documents, ineligibility, incorrect bank details, or submission after the deadline. You can appeal within 7 days with corrected documents.

Need Help with Your Refund?

If you have questions or face any issues with your refund claim, reach out to REAP authorities through:

📞 Contact Information

  • REAP Official Portal: Check "Help" or "Support" section for live chat/email support
  • Helpline Email: Submit your query with your application number and a detailed description
  • Portal Chat Window: Use the integrated chat feature to communicate with REAP coordinators
  • Official Website: Visit www.reap2026.in for latest updates and FAQs

💡 Pro Tips for Successful Refund Claim

  • Submit your refund claim as soon as you become eligible—don't wait until the deadline
  • Keep all documents organized and in digital format for quick submission
  • Use your registered email and mobile number for all REAP communications
  • Track your refund status regularly on the portal and maintain records of all submissions
  • If contacted by REAP for additional information, respond immediately to avoid rejection
